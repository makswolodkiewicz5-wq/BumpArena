\# 🕹️ BumpArena 2D



\*\*BumpArena 2D\*\* is a fast-paced arcade battle royale/sumo game. Your main objective is simple: push your opponent off the glowing platform!



\## 🎮 Controls (Local / 1v1 Mode)

\* \*\*Player 1 (Blue):\*\*

&#x20; \* Movement: `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` keys

&#x20; \* Ability / Dash: `Spacebar`

\* \*\*Player 2 (Red):\*\*

&#x20; \* Movement: Arrow keys (`Up`, `Down`, `Left`, `Right`)

&#x20; \* Ability / Dash: `Enter` or `Shift`



\## 🌐 Online Multiplayer (LAN \& Internet)

1\. \*\*Host:\*\* Click the "Create Room" button. Copy the generated \*\*Room Code\*\* and send it to your friend.

2\. \*\*Joiner:\*\* Paste the Room Code into the input field and click "Join Room".

3\. The game uses a peer-to-peer (P2P) WebRTC connection, meaning no heavy dedicated servers are required—play seamlessly over your local network (LAN) or across the internet!



\## ⚡ Power-ups \& Mechanics

\* Collect glowing power-ups spawning across the arena to gain tactical advantages (such as faster dashes, increased mass, shields, or enemy freezes).

\* Beware of the edges—the longer the match goes on, the harder it is to stay safe!
